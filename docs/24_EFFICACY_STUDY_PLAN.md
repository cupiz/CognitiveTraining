# 24 — Efficacy Study Plan

## Purpose

This document outlines the randomized controlled trial (RCT) design for evaluating training efficacy.

## Status

**Draft v1.0** — Created 2026-09-01

---

## 1. Research Question

Does 4 weeks of adaptive cognitive training produce greater improvement in cognitive performance compared to passive control?

## 2. Study Design

- **Type:** Randomized controlled trial (RCT)
- **Arms:** 2 (training vs. passive control)
- **Duration:** 4 weeks
- **Sessions:** 5 per week (20 total)
- **Follow-up:** 4 weeks post-training

## 3. Participants

### Sample Size
- **Power analysis:** 80% power, α = 0.05, expected d = 0.40
- **Minimum N:** 100 (50 per arm)
- **Target N:** 120 (60 per arm, accounting for 20% attrition)

### Inclusion Criteria
- Age 7–12 years
- Parental consent
- Child assent
- Access to mobile device
- Completed baseline assessment

### Exclusion Criteria
- Diagnosed cognitive disorder
- Current participation in other cognitive training
- < 80% session completion during baseline

## 4. Randomization

- **Method:** Stratified block randomization
- **Strata:** Age (7–9, 10–12), baseline performance (low, medium, high)
- **Block size:** 4, 6 (randomly varying)
- **Allocation:** Computer-generated, concealed

## 5. Interventions

### Training Group
- 5 sessions per week
- 15 minutes per session
- Adaptive difficulty (all 5 games)
- Feedback on performance

### Passive Control Group
- 5 sessions per week
- 15 minutes per session
- Fixed difficulty (D3)
- No performance feedback
- Sham progress indicators

## 6. Outcomes

### Primary Outcome
- Change in domain performance scores (composite)
- Measured at baseline, week 2, week 4, week 8

### Secondary Outcomes
- Change in individual domain scores
- Transfer to untrained tasks
- Session engagement metrics
- Adverse events

### Process Measures
- Session completion rate
- Time on task
- Game preferences
- Difficulty progression

## 7. Blinding

- **Participants:** Cannot be blinded (know if training is adaptive)
- **Assessors:** Blinded to group assignment
- **Analysts:** Blinded to group labels until analysis complete

## 8. Analysis Plan

### Primary Analysis
- Mixed ANOVA (group × time)
- Intention-to-treat (ITT)
- Per-protocol sensitivity analysis

### Secondary Analyses
- ANCOVA controlling for baseline
- Mediation analysis (mediators: engagement, difficulty progression)
- Subgroup analyses (age, baseline performance)

### Multiple Comparisons
- Bonferroni correction for primary outcomes
- False discovery rate (FDR) for secondary outcomes

## 9. Data Quality

- Real-time quality monitoring
- Automatic flagging of suspicious sessions
- Manual review of flagged data
- Pre-specified exclusion criteria

## 10. Ethical Considerations

- IRB approval required
- Parental consent required
- Child assent required
- Right to withdraw honored
- Data safety monitoring board
- Adverse event reporting
- No clinical claims

## 11. Timeline

| Phase | Duration | Activities |
|---|---|---|
| Preparation | 3 months | IRB, recruitment materials, platform setup |
| Recruitment | 2 months | Screen, consent, baseline |
| Intervention | 1 month | Training/control sessions |
| Follow-up | 1 month | Post-test, follow-up |
| Analysis | 2 months | Data cleaning, analysis, write-up |
| **Total** | **9 months** | |

## 12. Publication Plan

- Pre-register on OSF before data collection
- Report all pre-specified analyses
- Share data and code (de-identified)
- Follow CONSORT guidelines
- Disclose conflicts of interest

## 13. Limitations

- Cannot blind participants
- Hawthorne effects possible
- Device variability not controlled
- Generalizability limited to recruited sample
